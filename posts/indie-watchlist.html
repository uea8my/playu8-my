Indie watchlist for upcoming HTML5 titles

Small teams often bring the most distinctive ideas to browser games.

Published 2026-06-24

Smaller creators often experiment in ways that feel fresher than larger studios. The most interesting projects tend to combine smart mechanics with a clear, approachable hook.

What to watch

Look for unusual input ideas, strong themes, and loops that feel easy to understand quickly.

Support devs

Leave feedback, follow the work, and share projects when the concept feels worth seeing more of.
