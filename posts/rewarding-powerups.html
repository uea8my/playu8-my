Designing rewarding power-ups

Why thoughtful boosts feel exciting without breaking the rhythm of a game.

Published 2026-06-24

Power-ups work best when they add momentum and confidence rather than confusion. The most satisfying ones are easy to recognize, easy to use, and clearly worth the effort.

Give second chances

A shield or temporary safety net can encourage bold play without feeling punishing.

Create clear drama

Short bursts of slow motion or a sudden score boost can create a memorable moment when used sparingly.

Reward the right behavior

Multipliers and streak rewards feel stronger when they connect naturally to a player’s momentum and skill.
