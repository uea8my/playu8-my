Frequently asked questions

Here are clear answers to some of the most common questions about the experience, the site, and the games.

Is the experience easy to start?

Yes. The games are designed to feel approachable immediately, with simple controls and a clear sense of progress.

Does it work on mobile?

Absolutely. The site is built to stay responsive and comfortable on phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs directly in the browser, so you can jump in without downloading anything extra.

Are there hidden costs?

No. The experience is designed to be simple and accessible, with no extra purchase required to get started.

How are scores tracked?

Scores are saved locally in the browser so you can revisit and compare your progress later.

Can I share feedback?

Yes. We welcome suggestions and comments through the contact page.

What makes the site feel different?

The overall experience is focused on clarity, responsiveness, and a calm, polished feel.
